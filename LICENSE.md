## Questions this raises in practice

**Can I use it to build maps for a commercial game?**
Using the tool inside a paid commercial production is a commercial use of the tool and needs the
author's agreement. Ask him — it costs a message. The maps themselves remain your work.

**Can I fork it on GitHub and publish my version?**
Yes, keeping the attribution, stating your changes, and licensing your fork under CC BY-NC-SA
4.0.

**Can I ship it inside a free web app I host myself?**
Yes, if that app is not commercial and it carries the attribution, and if the whole derivative
is offered under the same license.

**Can I translate the interface and publish the result?**
Yes — that is exactly the ShareAlike case. Same license, credit kept, changes stated. The guide
explains how to add a locale.

**Can I remove the footer notice?**
No. It is the attribution the BY term requires. You may restyle or move it, as long as it stays
reasonably visible.
